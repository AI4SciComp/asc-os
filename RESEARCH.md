# ASC OS research contract

ASC OS develops generic research-state infrastructure. It owns contexts,
declared covers and overlaps, claims, evidence, decisions, lifecycle records,
deterministic restriction, gluing manifests, the CLI, and local stdio MCP.

It does not own numerical kernels, build policy, domain models, neural-operator
training, or publication prose. A declared cover is not a proof of global
correctness, and a verified claim records checked policy and evidence rather
than an automatic mathematical proof.
